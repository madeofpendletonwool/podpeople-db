Contributing

Adding people should take less time than reading this page. No account, no sign-up, no waiting for approval.

Add the people on a show

  1. Search for the show. Anything in the Podcast Index works, you can paste an RSS feed URL, and shows with no public feed can be added by name.
  2. In the Who's on this show? box, type a name and press Enter. Paste a list like “Alice, Bob and Carol” to add several at once.
  3. If the person already exists elsewhere, pick them from the suggestions. That links the shows together, so people following them see the new one.
  4. Hit Add. They appear on the page immediately.

The role defaults to Host. Pick Co-host, Guest or Producer if that fits better. Photos, links and bios are optional, and anyone can add them later from the person's page.

Add the guests on an episode

Open an episode from the show's page (or click + who's on this? under it) and add names the same way. Guests usually only appear on a few episodes, so crediting them per episode keeps their profiles accurate.

From Pinepods

If you use Pinepods, you don't need this site at all: podcast and episode pages have an Add people button that sends your additions here.

What happens next

Your addition goes live right away, marked unverified. A moderator reviews it later and either confirms it or rolls it back. Shows that tag people in their own feed (feed) are kept in sync automatically, and those credits can't be changed here; if they're wrong, the fix belongs in the feed.

Ground rules

  • Real people who are actually on the show. No jokes, no spam, no self-promotion on shows you're not on.
  • Use the name people know them by.
  • Only link photos you'd be comfortable seeing on an album cover.

Abuse gets reverted in one click, and repeat offenders get blocked. Everyone else: thank you!
